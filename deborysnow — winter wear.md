## What keeps you warm

Four materials do most of the work across the collection.

### Merino wool

Fine, soft and naturally temperature regulating for sweaters and scarves.

### Recycled down

Light fill for jackets that stay warm without the bulk.

### Boiled wool

Dense and wind resistant, used for our long coats.

### Brushed fleece

A soft inner layer for hats and collars.
